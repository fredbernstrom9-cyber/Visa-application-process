# AI use statement

The course allows AI "provided that students clearly cite the sources they used and explain how they used the tool, including the prompt or prompts entered". This statement covers that requirement.

## Tool

- **Claude** (Anthropic), used through **Claude Code**, on 24 September 2026.

## What we used it for

1. **Research:** web searches for current (2026) visa and study-permit rules in 8 EU countries and at EU level, with the sources recorded (see the website's *Sources & AI use* page and `assets/data.js`).
2. **Code:** writing the website (HTML, CSS, JavaScript) in this repository.
3. **Drafting analysis:** the new Environmental and Legal rows and all "Revised" columns of our PESTEL (`docs/PESTEL.md`), and first drafts of the other frameworks on the *Business case* page (Five Forces, VRIO, value proposition, SWOT/TOWS, Business Model Canvas, 4Ps, Ansoff, cost and revenue plan, organisation design, CAGE, SDGs).

## Limits of the AI output

- Several official government websites could not be opened directly from the AI's research environment, so some figures were confirmed through search results and secondary sources. These are marked **Verify** in the checker and must be checked by hand on the official site.
- Financial projections are assumptions, not data.
- Competitor descriptions are based on each company's public positioning and should be checked on their websites.
